# Pendulum + Clocks Sequence
*A 360° whole-body integration sequence (squat pendulum → clocks layer → cool-down hold)*

## Overview

This is a RageDance sequence for **whole-body coordination** without isolating parts. It begins close to the ground (supported squat + pendulum), then opens into **360° movement** (“clocks”)—including the **cardinal points** and every space between.

The aim is not perfect form. The aim is **flow with awareness**:
- balance and breath
- spine and legs working together
- circular motion (clockwise / counter-clockwise)
- shifting orientation in space (N / E / S / W and all angles between)
- listening to sensory change: sound, light, shadow, momentum

At first it can feel awkward. Once the flow arrives, it becomes smooth and enjoyable.

> Safety note (simple and honest): Warm up fully first. Move slowly. If anything feels sharp or unstable, reduce range, add support, switch to the seated version, or stop.
## The Pendulum Squat (Foundational Sequence)

This sequence begins close to the ground and uses support rather than force.

### Setup
- Begin after a full warm-up.
- Lower into a **deep squat**, resting comfortably on:
  - the toes,
  - the full foot,
  - or any combination that feels stable.
- Stability will differ from person to person.
- Elbows rest gently on the knees for support.

Balance should feel **available**, not strained.

### Pendulum Formation
- Extend the arms forward.
- Interlace the fingers loosely, forming a soft “ball.”
- This becomes the **pendulum**.

### Movement
- Begin to sway and explore.
- Let motion arise from the core, then involve the spine.
- Movement is slow, circular, and unforced.
- Explore:
  - side-to-side
  - forward and back
  - gentle rotation

Allow the spine to follow the pendulum rather than directing it.

### Rising Out of the Squat
- As the arms gradually lift, begin to rise.
- Hands may brace the knees at any point if there is wobble.
- If pain or instability appears:
  - reduce range,
  - slow down,
  - or pause.

### Seated Option
This entire sequence can be performed **seated**:
- Feet grounded
- Elbows may rest on thighs
- The same pendulum motion applies
