# Pendulum + Clocks Sequence
*A 360° whole-body integration sequence (squat pendulum → clocks layer → cool-down hold)*

## Overview

This is a RageDance sequence for **whole-body coordination** without isolating parts. It begins close to the ground (supported squat + pendulum), then opens into **360° movement** (“clocks”)—including the **cardinal points** and every space between.

The aim is not perfect form. The aim is **flow with awareness**:
- balance and breath
- spine and legs working together
- circular motion (clockwise / counter-clockwise)
- shifting orientation in space (N / E / S / W and all angles between)
- listening to sensory change: sound, light, shadow, momentum

At first it can feel awkward. Once the flow arrives, it becomes smooth and enjoyable.

> Safety note (simple and honest): Warm up fully first. Move slowly. If anything feels sharp or unstable, reduce range, add support, switch to the seated version, or stop.
